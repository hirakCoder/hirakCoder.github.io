01

Paste a user story or requirements page, pick the payment rail, and get structured test cases, either as a reviewer workbook or ready to upload into test management. It also converts hand-edited sheets.

02

One-click payment trace with audit-ready evidence

Follows a payment end to end, from the payment network through the database to the matching Kafka event, then writes the tracking record and a step-by-step evidence document with screenshots. Runs locally or from Jenkins.

Built with Copilot · Claude Opus

Produces valid bulk payment files (pain.001 payables, pain.013 requests, camt.056 cancellations) across transaction variants, with per-environment configuration so one scenario runs everywhere. Zero dependencies.

SwiftGen AI

Describe an app in plain English and get a working native iOS app: planned, written, compiled, repaired, launched in the simulator and streamed to your browser, then editable by chat. Built solo: 315 commits in 3.5 months, ~90k lines.

  1. Understand. Intent classification and complexity rating route each request.
  2. Generate. A task-aware router across Claude Sonnet/Opus, Gemini, Grok and GPT, with fallbacks, retries and truncation recovery. Complex apps are built screen by screen with schema-enforced output.
  3. Pre-validategate ~970 injected rules and a 193-pattern validator fix known failure modes before any build, without calling an LLM.
  4. Build & healgate Compile; on failure an LLM healer gets 3 attempts, escalating to a stronger model.
  5. Verifygate Launch in the iOS Simulator, screenshot, process-alive and crash-log checks.
  6. Learn. 600+ healer fixes logged; recurring ones get promoted into the validator.

How I work with AI

AI output is verified, not trusted.

Fix the system, not the output

Never hand-patch what an AI generated. Fix the prompt, the validator or the healer, then re-test across simple, medium and complex cases.

Build success ≠ working

A green build proves nothing. Launch it, tap it, screenshot it, check the crash logs.

Measure, then claim

Every number on this page comes from logs or live data, with the date and model generation stated.

Independent oracles

Check AI-written logic against a source it didn't write, such as official tables, specs or a second system.

HouseNest — Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026 · Contact: hirak.b@hotmail.com

HouseNest is a personal household budgeting application. This policy explains what data it handles and where that data lives. The short version: your financial data stays on your own device.

What data HouseNest handles

When you connect a bank account through Plaid, HouseNest receives your transaction history (dates, amounts, merchant names, categories) and basic account labels (account type and last four digits). You can also type expenses in by hand.

Where it is stored

All financial data is stored exclusively on your own iOS device, protected by iOS hardware encryption and your device passcode/Face ID. Bank access tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. HouseNest's server component is a stateless relay that passes API requests to Plaid and stores nothing — no transactions, no tokens, no logs of your financial data.

What is shared, and with whom

  • Plaid connects to your bank on your behalf. Your bank credentials go to Plaid/your bank directly and are never visible to HouseNest. Plaid's own privacy policy is at plaid.com/legal, and you can manage or revoke connections at my.plaid.com.
  • AI analysis (optional). If you ask for an advisor analysis, aggregated monthly totals (category sums, budget pace, subscription totals, top merchant totals) are processed transiently to generate the analysis. Individual transactions are never sent, and nothing is stored server-side.
  • No one else. HouseNest does not sell, share, or transfer your data to any other party, and contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only on your device and only as long as you keep it. You can delete individual entries, erase everything with the in-app "start fresh" control, or delete the app to remove all data. Unlinking a bank deletes its access token.

Changes

SwiftGen AI

Multi-agent iOS app generator · solo · prepared for Y Combinator

You describe an app in one sentence. SwiftGen plans it, writes native SwiftUI, compiles it, repairs what breaks, launches it in the iOS Simulator and streams it to your browser, where you keep changing it by chat.

I built it alone: 315 commits in three and a half months, about 90,000 lines. The interesting part isn't the generation. It's everything that stops a model's output from reaching a user unchecked.

  1. 01Classify. Intent and complexity decide the route and the model.
  2. 02Generate. A router across Claude Sonnet and Opus, Gemini, Grok and GPT, with fallbacks and truncation recovery.
  3. 03Pre-validate. About 970 injected rules and 193 fix patterns, no LLM call.gate
  4. 04Build and heal. Three repair attempts, escalating to a stronger model.gate
  5. 05Verify. Launch, screenshot, process check, crash-log scan.gate
  6. 06Learn. 600+ repairs logged; repeat fixes promoted into the validator.

§3Method

AI output gets verified, not trusted

1

Fix the system, not the output

Never hand-patch what a model wrote. Fix the prompt, the validator or the healer, then re-test simple, medium and complex cases.

2

A green build proves nothing

Launch it, tap it, screenshot it, read the crash log.

3

Measure, then claim

Every number here comes from logs or live data, with its date and the models it ran on.

4

Use an outside oracle

Check AI-written logic against something it didn't write: official tables, the spec, a second system.

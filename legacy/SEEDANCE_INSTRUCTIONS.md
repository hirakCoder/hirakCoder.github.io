# Generate Portfolio Videos — Seedance 2.0 (5 minutes)

Go to: https://dreamina.capcut.com/ai-tool/video-generator

## Video 1: HERO BACKGROUND (required)

1. Upload `seedance-reference-hero.png` as first frame
2. Settings: 16:9, 1080p, max duration (15 sec)
3. Paste this prompt:

```
Cinematic slow-motion silhouette of a person standing in a dark room, backlit by volumetric blue and purple light beams streaming through fog. The person is seen from behind, slightly off-center, looking upward. Floating luminous particles drift slowly around them. Deep shadows, dramatic rim lighting on the silhouette edges. Subtle camera drift forward. Film grain texture, moody atmospheric, anamorphic lens flare. Dark ambient. Professional cinematography.
```

4. Generate → Download → Save as `hero-silhouette.mp4`

## KQ1. Which roles actually exist in Canada (or Canada-remote) at the five companies, with location policy, level and pay where shown

### Takeaway
As of 2026-10-13 there are **zero** postings titled Test Engineer / SDET / QAE / "Software Engineer, Tools and Infrastructure" in Canada at Google, Amazon, Microsoft or Meta; the only Canadian QE-titled openings at the five companies are **three Apple SDET/Quality Engineer roles, all in Vancouver** (two of which explicitly ask for applied-LLM test-generation experience). The realistic Canadian entry points are therefore generalist SWE/"Software Developer" postings (Google Waterloo/Toronto, Microsoft Vancouver, Amazon Toronto/Vancouver), Microsoft's customer-facing architect roles in Toronto (one explicitly 2 days/week in office), and the Apple Vancouver QE roles.

## KQ2. Interview loops and what they test (Google TE/SETI vs SWE; Microsoft SDE; Amazon QAE/SDET; Apple SQA)

### Takeaway
Only Google and Amazon publish process detail; Google's official page says the whole process "typically" takes "roughly 6-8 weeks", uses structured rubric-based panels, "no brain teasers", and bans AI tools in interviews, while Amazon's official SDE II page specifies a 90-minute OA, four 55-minute loop interviews and an outcome within 5 business days. Everything about Google TE-vs-SWE bars, Microsoft's "As Appropriate" round and Apple's QE loop comes from third-party or first-person sources and must be treated as anecdotal.

### Cited Findings

**Google (official)**
- "While the process may differ slightly for each role or team, the same basics apply... Typically, you can expect it to take roughly 6-8 weeks and to include some of the steps below." — [Google Careers, "Our hiring process"](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/how-we-hire/)
- Steps listed: "Assessments: Before moving into interviews, you may be asked to take the Google Hiring Assessment, designed to measure workstyle skills... You might also be asked to complete additional role-related assessments, like a coding exercise"; "Recruiter conversations: ...one or two shorter conversations over phone or video"; "Project work: Depending on the type of role, we may ask candidates to complete a small project"; "Interviews: ...a panel of interviews, either over video or in person with a rotating cast of Googlers... There aren't any brain teasers either." — [same page](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/how-we-hire/)
- "Structured interviewing: Every candidate is assessed using clear rubrics. We use the same rubrics for everyone being considered for that role"; "Open-ended questions: We ask role-related, open-ended questions to learn how you solve problems" — [same page](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/how-we-hire/)
- "Important note: ...AI tools are not permitted to be used during your interviews." — [same page](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/how-we-hire/)
- "you can apply for up to three jobs every 30 days"; "Most Googlers applied for other roles at Google before they eventually made it to interviews" — [same page](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/how-we-hire/)
- Decision: "we take into account a number of perspectives to reach a decision. If we decide that you're the best candidate for the role, your recruiter will reach out to you with an offer." — [same page](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/how-we-hire/)
- Canadian postings add: "In most instances, this position requires in-person interviews as part of the hiring process." and "Google utilizes AI tools to assist in assessing candidates in our hiring processes." — [Android Infrastructure Software Developer posting](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/142151738999087814)

**Google (third-party / anecdotal — not official)**
- Third-party prep site (2025): "Google's interview process runs 6 to 12 weeks from application to offer"; recruiter screen ~30 min; technical phone screen 45–60 min with 1–2 coding problems; onsite 4–5 rounds (2–3 coding, 1 system design for L4+, 1 behavioral assessing "General Cognitive Ability, Role-Related Knowledge, Googleyness, and Leadership"); hiring committee "review your interview packet without having met you"; offers "two to six weeks" after onsite — [Final Round AI, Google interview process](https://www.finalroundai.com/blog/google-interview-process) (third-party; not verified)

You will write real code against a real algorithmic bar"; Software Engineers in Test "face near-full SWE coding loops; Test Engineers handle lighter but still genuine coding weighted toward strategy and product quality"; "an independent hiring committee that never met you reads the packet and decides" — [QAJobFit, Google Test Engineer interview](https://qajobfit.com/resources/google-test-engineer-interview-questions) (third-party; not verified)

### Inferences
- For this candidate, Google is a SWE loop (DS&A coding at the L4/L5 bar, a system-design round, Googleyness), not a test-design loop, because there is no TE/SETI opening in Canada to be slotted into; the third-party claim that SET loops are "near-full SWE" is consistent with that.
- Amazon's QAE loop (if a Canadian QAE role appeared) is the lowest coding bar of the five by the one first-person account (easy-level arrays/strings/hashmaps), but the account is a single 2024 anecdote.
- Microsoft's official "2-4 conversations" understates what candidates report (4–5 rounds plus an AA), so prep should assume 5–6 sessions.
- Java is acceptable everywhere a language is mentioned (Google TE III lists Python/Java/C++; Microsoft lists Java; Amazon states no restriction; Apple App Store QE lists Java), so Python fluency is not a gate for the loops themselves, only for the two Apple Creativity Apps SDET postings that ask for Python/Swift/JS.

- No official statement from Google on which languages are allowed in coding interviews was found on the hiring page (the page does not list languages).
- No official or credible description of a Google "team match" step was retrieved from a primary page (third-party pages mention hiring committee; team match is not described on the official page).
- Apple's SQA loop: no official or retrievable anecdotal detail; Glassdoor pages blocked.
- Microsoft's official page does not describe the hiring manager's role or the AA round.
- Blind, Reddit (JSON blocked) and Glassdoor (HTTP 403) could not be read; no 2025–2026 first-person Google TE loop reports were obtained.
